Organising a community gathering, cultural program, club event, workshop, sporting event or social meetup?

You may already be using an online form to collect names and phone numbers. That works for collecting responses -- but organising an event usually involves much more.

With **Desify**, you can create your event, collect registrations, ask attendees the questions you need, control who can register, send email notifications, manage your attendee list and check people in on the day -- all in one place.

## Create Your RSVP Event in Three Easy Steps

### Step 1 -- Add Your Event Details

Add your **event title, category, description, date, time, venue and address**.

You can also select the **time zone**, which is helpful for events across Australia, set a **recurring event** if needed, and upload images to make your event page more engaging.

![Step 1: Event Details](https://res.cloudinary.com/dtvrbdvkx/image/upload/v1787566833/EventDetails_yk9epw.png)

### Step 2 -- Add Organiser Details

Add your **organiser or organisation name**, along with contact details such as **phone, email and website**.

You can also include **social media links** and add **event coordinators** who are helping organise or manage the event.

![Step 2: Organiser details, social links and coordinators](https://res.cloudinary.com/dtvrbdvkx/image/upload/v1787569332/organiser_dlhiri.png)

### Step 3 -- Configure Your RSVP

#### Set Capacity and Booking Limits

Set your **event capacity** to control how many people can attend, and a **booking limit** to control how many attendees can be included in a single registration.

#### Create Custom Registration Questions

Every event needs different information from its attendees.

Desify gives organisers **SIX** flexible question types:

* **Text** -- attendee types the answer
* **Dropdown** -- pick one from a list
* **Multiple choice** -- pick one, round buttons
* **Checkboxes** -- pick many, tick all that apply
* **Linear scale** -- pick a number, 1 to 5
* **Date** -- pick a date, can include time

Use them to collect exactly what your event needs -- such as dietary requirements, meal preferences, interests, attendance dates, membership details or eligibility information.

![Step 3: Capacity, booking limit and custom questions](https://res.cloudinary.com/dtvrbdvkx/image/upload/v1787570241/questions_wmrfd2.png)

#### Require Important Information

Mark any question as **Required to register**. If an attendee skips it, Desify prompts them to **Fill Required Info** before they can continue.

![Required question and Fill Required Info prompt](https://res.cloudinary.com/dtvrbdvkx/image/upload/v1787569477/Required_w8phvy.png)

#### Block Registration Based on an Answer

You can also block registration when an attendee does not meet your event requirements.

For example, if you ask **"Have you received an invitation to this event?"** and the attendee selects **No**, Desify can stop the registration and display: **"This is an invitation-only event."**

![Block registration: organiser setup and attendee blocked result](https://res.cloudinary.com/dtvrbdvkx/image/upload/v1787569475/Block_Registration_wau0jn.png)

## A Better Registration Experience for Attendees

Attendees can register directly from your event page either by signing in to their Desify account or continuing as a guest. They can enter their details, answer the questions you configured and, where your booking limit allows it, add additional attendees to the same registration.

Email notifications help keep attendees and organisers informed throughout the RSVP process.

![Attendee registration form with custom questions](https://res.cloudinary.com/dtvrbdvkx/image/upload/v1787569519/attendeexperience_acjibx.png)

## Manage Check-In on Event Day

Your RSVP list becomes your **event-day check-in list**.

Search for attendees, see who has arrived and mark them as **Checked In** directly from the organiser dashboard.

![Attendee check-in dashboard](https://res.cloudinary.com/dtvrbdvkx/image/upload/v1787569554/Check-in_wicojb.png)

You can quickly view: **Not Checked In -> Checked In -> All Attendees**

No separate attendee sheet is needed at the door.

## Download Your Attendee Data

Need your registration information outside Desify?

Download your attendee list as a **CSV file** for Excel, reporting or your own records.

![Download CSV option](https://res.cloudinary.com/dtvrbdvkx/image/upload/v1787569553/Downloadcsv_owr05i.png)

You loved it once. The lehenga from a wedding two seasons ago. The sherwani worn for one big day. The saree that was perfect for that one function, and the kids' festival outfits they've already grown out of. Beautiful pieces, barely worn, sitting folded at the back of the wardrobe.

Someone near you is looking for exactly that.

## Loved it once, let someone love it now

Good ethnic wear is made to be worn, not stored. A little effort turns that pile into cash and gives a gorgeous outfit a second life. Somebody finds the perfect piece for their next function, you get your space back, and nothing beautiful goes to waste.

## Why list your ethnic wear on Desify

- **Give fashion a second life.** A lehenga, kurta or saree you've moved on from becomes someone else's favourite.
- **Declutter your space.** Clear the wardrobe of outfits you'll never wear again.
- **Keep it out of landfill.** Perfectly good clothes stay in use instead of the bin.
- **Earn extra.** Turn unworn ethnic wear into money in your pocket.

And it's completely free to list — no fees to post, no catch.

## How it works

- Take a clear photo in good light.
- Write a short, honest description — size, fabric, condition, occasion.
- Set a fair price, or list it free to give away.
- Reply to the people who message you, and arrange a pickup.

That's it. The outfit at the back of your wardrobe deserves a new home. Give it one today.

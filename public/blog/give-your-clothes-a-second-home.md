We all have them. The jeans that don't fit anymore. The jacket you wore twice. The kids' clothes they grew out of in a season. They sit at the back of the wardrobe, taking up space, doing nothing.

Here's the thing — someone near you is looking for exactly that.

## Better than the bin

Throwing good clothes away feels wrong, and it is. A little effort turns that pile into cash and keeps perfectly good clothes out of landfill. Somebody gets a bargain, you get your wardrobe back, and nothing useful gets wasted.

## Why list them on Desify

- **It's free.** No fees to post, no catch.
- **Local buyers.** People in your own suburb, no posting or shipping to deal with.
- **Takes minutes.** Snap a photo, write a line, done.
- **Real community.** You're selling to families around you, not strangers across the world.

## How it works

1. Take a clear photo in good light.
2. Write a short, honest description — size, brand, condition.
3. Set a fair price, or list it free to give away.
4. Reply to the people who message you, and arrange a pickup.

That's it. The jacket at the back of your wardrobe deserves a second home. Give it one today.

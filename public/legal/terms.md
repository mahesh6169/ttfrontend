#### 4.1.1 Event Creation
Users can create Free/RSVP events on Desify.  
Event organisers are responsible for ensuring the accuracy of event details and compliance with all relevant laws.  
Desify does not host or run the event and is not responsible for its delivery.

Desify may send email notifications related to Free/RSVP events, including but not limited to:

- Registration confirmations and cancellations  
- Updates to critical event details  
- Notifications of event deletion  

These notifications are intended to keep organisers and attendees informed about the status of the event.

#### 4.1.3 Event Expiry
- **Single Events** expire automatically **5 days after the event date**  
- **Recurring Events** expire **5 days after the last event in the series**  
- Once expired, events are marked as “expired” and remain visible but cannot accept new registrations

#### 4.1.4 Recurring Events
Recurring events consist of a parent event and one or more child events.  
When the parent event is deleted, all child events are automatically deleted.

#### 4.1.5 Moderation and Removal Rights
Desify administrators or moderators may remove events that are reported for abuse, scams, illegal content, or other violations of these Terms.  
We may also remove events for operational or technical reasons to maintain platform quality.

#### 4.1.6 Event Deletion
Organisers may delete their Free/RSVP events at any time.  
When a Free/RSVP event is deleted (by the organiser or Desify):

- The event details and images are permanently removed  
- All registrations are deleted  
- All related chat messages and conversations are deleted  
- The event is removed from all user wishlists  

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.



### 4.2 Paid Events

#### 4.2.1 Event Creation and Expiry
Paid events follow the same creation process as Free/RSVP events.  
They expire 5 days after the event date, and once expired, are marked as “expired” and remain visible for reference but no longer accept ticket sales.

#### 4.2.2 Notifications
Paid events follow the same notification flow as Free/RSVP events, including event creation, registration confirmation, cancellations, updates, and deletion notices.

#### 4.2.3 Event Deletion
Paid events may be removed after consultation with the organiser.  
When a paid event is deleted:
- The event details and images are permanently removed  
- Messages and conversations are deleted  
- The event is removed from wishlists  
- **Financial records (tickets, invoices) are retained for at least 2 years** for compliance and accounting purposes  

#### 4.2.4 Organiser Access
Organisers retain access to check-in data and ticketing records during the active period of the event and for a reasonable time after expiry for reconciliation.

Platform fees are non-refundable, even if the event is cancelled.
